Policyholders who file a home or auto claim often don't know what is happening with it.
They call the contact centre to ask "where is my claim?", wait on hold, and many of
them are older customers who are not comfortable with apps.

Idea: proactively keep every claimant informed. Send a short text message or email
whenever the claim moves to a new stage (received, adjuster assigned, inspection booked,
documents needed, payment approved, paid), with a plain-language explanation of what
happens next and what, if anything, the customer needs to do. Customers can reply to
ask a question or upload a missing document from a secure link, without logging in to
a portal.

It must respect contact preferences and consent, work for customers with accessibility
needs, and give claims handlers a view of what each customer was told and when.
